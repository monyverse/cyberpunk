SPEXI 

🧩
Challenge Overview
Spexi is building the next generation of aerial imagery capture leveraging blockchain technology. By creating a decentralized ecosystem for drone enthusiasts, Spexi and the LayerDrone Foundation enable high resolution imagery to be able to capture with minimal lead time and hyper localized capture; something that both aircraft and satellite technologies fail to optimize around. In order to demonstrate the value of the imagery captured, Spexi needs to grow its imagery derivative database. What does this mean? There are hundreds of use cases of aerial imagery, from planning disaster response during a hurricane to optimizing the location of a solar panel installation on a roof. Many different types of imagery derivative products help get customers to those answers quicker, through solutions such as orthomosaics, 3d point cloud, elevation models, 3d mesh, and more recently, radiance fields (e.g. Gaussian Splatting). Many of these capabilities can be resource intensive though. We’d like to explore what are some low-cost AI-powered solutions to help our customers respond quicker to a multitude of scenarios with richer insights?

Example project ideas
Orbit Views from Panorama Views: In aerial imagery, you can position a camera and swivel 360 degrees to create a panorama, or you can pick a point to circle around where the camera is always facing the center of the circle (orbit). Given various points of panorama captures in a region, is it possible to artificially create orbit views of selected features?
Change Detection and Identification: Capturing change between two timestamps of the same region is a valuable problem in aerial imagery. Change detection means capturing where change has occurred, but not necessarily what specifically has changed. Change identification on the other hand informs us exactly what has changed, so for example, a new building has appeared or the condition of the tree canopy has degraded. Change identification is usually more meaningful but requires more processing. What is a low cost way to extract change identification from a region of capture?
Geometrically Accurate VLM: VLM’s (Vision Language Models) are extensions of LLM’s that incorporate imagery. In the aerial imagery domain specifically, the geometric information within those pixels are extremely valuable. While VLM’s are rapidly improving, they’re still not great when it comes to metric information within the imagery. Is it possible to train or augment an existing model that retains this type of information so that a query such as 'what is the square footage of the roof' can be reasonably accurate?
✅
Submission Requirements
Must provide a working demo or prototype
Must include documentation explaining the use of Spexi’s imagery
Must provide open and public source code
Must disclose use of 3rd party data sources if leveraged
✅
